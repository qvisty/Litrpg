# The System — Design Document

The full mechanics of the hidden game system, including things the characters never learn.
This is the *author's* reference. The reader only ever meets what a character has earned the
right to know (see `src/appendix-system.md` for the spoiler-safe, in-story version).

> **Status:** Draft / provisional. The *origin* of the system is an OPEN QUESTION (see the
> story bible) and is intentionally left blank here. Everything below is written so it stays
> compatible with any origin we later choose.

---

## Design pillars

1. **Hidden.** Only a chosen few perceive it. To everyone else, a "player" is just an
   unusually capable, unusually haunted person.
2. **Rewards humanity, not slaughter.** Progress comes from courage, cunning, endurance,
   leadership, mercy — choices, not kill-counts. Killing earns little or nothing.
3. **It costs something.** The more a player leans on the system, the more detached from
   their own humanity they feel. Power and numbness rise together.
4. **Medium crunch.** Stats, skills and levels are real and legible, but the prose leads —
   numbers appear when they sharpen a moment, never to fill space.
5. **Grounded.** No fireballs. Bonuses read as being *slightly* faster, steadier, luckier,
   more persuasive than a human should be — deniable, uncanny, never cinematic magic.

## Attributes

| Attribute | What it governs | How it reads on the page |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| **Nerve** | Fear control under fire | Hands that don't shake when they should |
| **Cunning** | Reading situations, traps, deception | Seeing the angle in time to use it |
| **Endurance** | Physical and mental stamina | Still standing when the body should have quit |
| **Influence** | Leadership, persuasion, presence | Others move because you asked |

*(Open: do we want a fifth, e.g. **Insight/Luck**? Decide when the first system reveal is
written.)*

## Skills

Learned by doing, not bought. A skill is named the moment the system first recognizes it.
Examples (provisional): *Demolitions, Field Medicine, Marksmanship, Cold Reading, Forgery,
Survival, Command.* Skills have ranks; ranks unlock subtle real-world competence, never
super-powers.

## Leveling & "the cost"

- XP is awarded for **meaningful action under stakes** — and the system's verdict on what was
  "meaningful" is itself a small, recurring mystery (it rewards mercy oddly often).
- Each level grants attribute/skill growth **and** deepens the detachment. Mechanically we may
  model this as a rising **Distance** value: high Distance = sharper performance, duller
  feeling. A core tragedy: the better you get, the less you feel the thing you're fighting
  for.

## The interface

How does a player *see* it? (Provisional — lock in Chapter 1.) Candidate: a faint overlay
only they perceive, surfacing in moments of stakes — not a constant HUD. Terse, cold,
administrative wording, like a ledger kept by something that does not love you.

## What players can learn about each other

When two players are near, something registers — a flicker, a wrongness, a notification.
This is the mechanical seed of the convergence plot and of the midway death's revelation.
