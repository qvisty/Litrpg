# A Note Before You Begin

Everything in these pages that can be checked against the record is true to it. The dates, the
fronts, the cold, the fear, the names of operations. They happened, and people died in them who
were braver and more frightened than any character invented here.

One thing is invented. A handful of people in this war could see something the rest of us could
not, a quiet reckoning that watched what they did and kept the count. They did not ask for it.
They could not turn it off. And for a long time, each of them believed they were the only one.

This is a story about what such a thing would cost a person, and about the ones who carried it
anyway.
